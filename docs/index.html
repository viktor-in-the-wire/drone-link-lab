DLP 1.0 / MAVLink 2 framing / HMAC-SHA256

Drone Link Lab

A telemetry link between a simulated drone and a ground station. Every frame is checked by a CRC and signed with HMAC-SHA256, built from real bytes, live in your browser.

§01LIVE LINK · DRONE ⇄ GROUND
01

Fly it. Every frame is checked.

The drone sends a signed HEARTBEAT at 1 Hz and DRONE_TELEMETRY at 5 Hz. The ground station runs every frame through the seven receiver checks. Fly with the sticks or the keyboard, then open any frame.

Share of radio frames dropped or corrupted in transit.
§02RED TEAM · SIMULATED
02

Break the link. Watch it hold.

The attacker can hear the link, record frames and inject its own. It knows the format and the CRC, but not the key. Launch an attack and follow the frame through the receiver, then switch a defence off and try again. Every attack touches only the simulated link on this page.
